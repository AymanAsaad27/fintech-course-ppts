# FinTech Course Wiki

Welcome to the FinTech course presentation repository. This wiki provides links to slides, upload instructions, and maintainer guidance.

## Quick links

- [Presentation index](../README.md#presentation-index): Browse by topic, presenter, or group.
- [All slides and PDFs](../presentations/): Browse or download contributed files.
- [Upload guide](../CONTRIBUTING.md): Submit your presentation through a browser.
- [Questions and feedback](https://github.com/soxee1/fintech-course-ppts/issues): Report errors, broken links, or upload problems.
- [Maintainer guide](./MAINTAINING.md): Instructions for collecting and updating materials.

## How submissions are collected

Upload slides → Open a pull request → Maintainer merges it → Update both homepage indexes.

Slides and PDFs are stored in the main repository's `presentations/` directory. The wiki provides guidance and navigation.

## Reading and downloading

No sign-in is needed to browse. Download slides and open them in PowerPoint, WPS, or Keynote. PDFs can be previewed online.

Copyright remains with the original authors. Credit the author and source when citing; other uses require the author's permission unless otherwise stated.
